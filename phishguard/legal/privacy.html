What PhishGuard does

PhishGuard checks emails and links for phishing. When you click Check for phishing in Outlook, or when your organization connects a mailbox, PhishGuard analyzes the message and returns a risk verdict.

Data we process

  • Message content of the emails you choose to check: sender, recipients, subject, headers, body, links, and attachment names, types and sizes. Attachment contents are not analyzed.
  • Settings such as an organization access key, stored in your own mailbox settings by Microsoft Outlook.
  • Technical logs such as request time, IP address and error codes, used to run and secure the service.

How we use it

We use message content only to produce a phishing verdict for you. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. By default, PhishGuard does not store the content of checked messages after the verdict is returned.

AI processing

To improve accuracy, message content may be sent to Anthropic, PBC, which provides the Claude AI model, for analysis. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which do not allow using customer API data to train its models. Your organization can turn AI analysis off, in which case only PhishGuard's rule-based checks run.

Where data is processed

PhishGuard runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in {{HOSTING_REGION}}. AI analysis is processed by Anthropic in the United States.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to the service can be restricted with an organization access key.

Your choices and rights
