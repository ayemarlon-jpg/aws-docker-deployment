How to check an email

  1. Open the email in Outlook (desktop, web or mobile).
  2. Select Check for phishing in the ribbon, or in the … / Apps menu.
  3. Read the verdict: Looks safe, Be careful or Phishing detected, with the reasons.

Pin the pane (pin icon) to check every email you open automatically.

What should I do with a phishing email?

Don't click links, open attachments or reply. Report it to your IT or security team, then delete it.

It says I need an access key

Your organization protects its PhishGuard service with a key. Ask your IT admin for it, then open Settings in the PhishGuard pane.

I think the verdict is wrong

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with a short description. Please don't forward the suspicious email itself unless we ask.

For administrators

Admins can deploy PhishGuard to everyone from the Microsoft 365 admin center: Settings → Integrated apps → Get apps, search for PhishGuard.
